Delivery

Lagos: delivery is typically 1 - 3 working days after dispatch.

Nigeria: nationwide delivery is typically 3 - 7 working days after dispatch.

International: available on request. Delivery timing and customs charges are confirmed before payment.

Ready-to-wear pieces are dispatched in 3 - 5 working days. Bespoke pieces have an agreed production timeline.

Returns

Please inspect your delivery promptly. Contact us within 48 hours if an item arrives damaged or incorrect. Bespoke and worn items cannot be returned. For eligible ready-to-wear returns, the item must be unworn, with tags, and returned within 7 days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I pay?

Paystack or Flutterwave payment options will be enabled at checkout before launch. Until then, the team confirms payment securely by WhatsApp.

Can I order by WhatsApp?

Yes. Use the WhatsApp button on every product to ask questions or place an order directly.

Can you make a custom size?

Yes. Use the bespoke form or WhatsApp us your measurements, preferred fabric, colour and deadline.

Do you have a physical location?

Add your Lagos showroom or pick-up address here before launch. Appointments are recommended for fittings.

Privacy

We use contact and order information only to fulfil orders and communicate about Zyamsk. Add your final provider-specific privacy statement before collecting payments or newsletter subscribers.

Terms

Prices are in Nigerian naira and may change before an order is confirmed. Bespoke production begins after payment and approval of the final brief.
